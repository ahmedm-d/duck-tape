- `model-training/src/training/utils.py` — streaming environment loader (`model-training/.env.training`), generic deterministic train/eval splitter (`seed=42`), unified diff path/symbol extractors, and UTF-8 LF JSONL writers (`write_instruction_jsonl`, `write_chat_jsonl`, `write_preference_jsonl`).

### Source Provenance & Scrubbing Summary
- **`router`**: Streamed from `SWE-bench/SWE-smith` (750) and `SWE-Gym/SWE-Gym` (650); 77 secret redactions applied. Covers all 10 routing destinations in both train and eval splits. Note: primary `router` training set is generated in the Adaption Web UI; this local dataset provides the 200-row held-out benchmark and 1,200-row fallback backup.
- **`ticket_parser`**: Topped up to 5,000 train + 200 eval from `princeton-nlp/SWE-bench_Verified` (`test` split, 480), `SWE-Gym/SWE-Gym` (2,200), and `cjsanjay/jira_train_17012024` (2,520); 7 secret redactions applied.
- **`commit_pr_writer`**: Topped up to 5,000 train + 200 eval from `bigcode/commitpackft` (`python`: 1,900, `typescript`: 1,900) and `Maxscha/commitbench` (1,400); 8 secret redactions applied. Every completion verified to contain `### Summary`, `### Cited Symbols`, `### Confidence Assessment`, and `### Risk Assessment`.
- **`log_classifier`**: Topped up to 5,000 train + 200 eval from `logpai/loghub` structured CSVs (1,200), `TestingResearchIllinois/idoft` (`py-data.csv` & `pr-data.csv`, 1,800), and `SWE-Gym/SWE-Gym` + `SWE-bench/SWE-smith` (2,200); 0 secret hits detected.
- **`implementer`**: Streamed resolved (`resolved == 1` or `True`) trajectories from `nebius/SWE-rebench-openhands-trajectories` (1,700), `SWE-bench/SWE-smith-trajectories` (`tool` split, 2,100), and `R2E-Gym/R2EGym-SFT-Trajectories` (1,400); normalized `tool_calls[].function.arguments` into scrubbed JSON dicts (2,579 train / 103 eval trajectories with tool calls), dropped 9,284 unresolved, 2,407 >45,000-token, and 2,694 duplicate trajectories; 346 secret redactions applied. Max combined trajectory tokens: 44,986 (train) / 44,900 (eval).
- **`test_writer`**: Streamed from `nebius/SWE-rebench-openhands-trajectories` filtered by `gen_tests_correct >= 1` (600), `kjain14/testgeneval` (1,189), `SWE-Gym/SWE-Gym` (1,400), and `SWE-bench/SWE-smith` (2,011); 102 secret redactions applied. Every completion verified to contain `### Reproduction Test Diff` and `### Verification Command`.
- **`reviewer`**: Streamed from `fasterinnerlooper/codereviewer` (Hugging Face mirror of `microsoft/CodeReviewer`: `train_generation` 2,200 + 400 top-up, `train_refinement` 1,600) and `Dahoas/code-review-instruct-critique-revision-python` (1,000); 32 secret redactions applied. Formatted as DPO `PreferenceRow` pairs contrasting symbol/line-grounded reviews against realistic hard-negative reviews (style-only nitpicks, hallucinated line numbers, and unverified rewrite claims).
- **`security_reviewer`**: Streamed from `colin/PrimeVul` (1,200 exploitable), `MickyMike/cvefixes_bigvul` (700 exploitable + 700 benign/patched), and `bstee615/diversevul` (700 exploitable + 1,900 benign) paired with 97 live OSV advisories queried from `api.osv.dev`; 1 secret redaction applied. Enforces Steward's security boundary ("A dependency version match alone is not proof of exploitability") with strictly balanced 2,500/2,500 train and 100/100 eval splits.
